        Questions

        What can I type?
        Numbers, x, + − * / and ^ (or **), brackets, pi or π, e, and the functions sin, cos, tan, their inverses (asin or arcsin), sinh, cosh, tanh, sec, csc, cot, sqrt, cbrt, abs or |x|, ln, log (base 10), log2, exp, floor, ceil, round, sign, min, max and pow. Multiplication can be implied, so 2x, 3sin(x) and 2(x + 1) all work. You can also start with y = or f(x) =.
        How do sliders work?
        Any single letter other than x and e is a parameter. The first time you use one, it gets a slider from −10 to 10 with a step of 0.1. Change the range and step to suit, or press play to sweep it back and forth. A letter shared by two functions is the same slider in both.
        How accurate are the numbers?
        Integrals use Simpson's rule with 4,000 intervals, roots and intersections are refined by bisection to about 13 significant figures, and slopes use a central difference. For smooth functions the results match exact answers to well beyond the digits shown.
        Where is my graph saved?
        In your browser's storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account. Your last graph is there when you come back.
        How do I share a graph?
        Use Copy link. The link holds your functions, colours, slider values, view and overlays, so whoever opens it sees the same graph. Export PNG saves an image with a legend instead.
        Does it work on a phone?
        Yes. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and use the insert row for symbols like √, π and |x| that are awkward on a phone keyboard.
